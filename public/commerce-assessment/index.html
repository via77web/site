COMMERCE ASSESSMENT

Find out what to fix first on your ecommerce website.

The Commerce Assessment is a human-led review of your store. It leaves you with a ranked list of what to do next, whether or not you work with us afterwards.

What is the Commerce Assessment?

A structured review of how customers move through your store, how well it is measured and how the technology holds up. It is not an automated score. We agree the scope, access, timing and any fees with you before the review begins.

What we need from you

  • Your store URL and a short description of your goals.
  • Read access to analytics, and to Search Console if available.
  • Shopify or platform collaborator access, if the review covers the admin and apps.
  • Anything you already know is a problem, and any constraints such as trading dates.

Areas reviewed

AreaWhat we look at
Customer journeyFinding, comparing and choosing products, on phones especially
Product and collection pagesClarity, filters, variants and the information people need
Cart and checkoutFriction, delivery information and payment options within platform limits
PerformancePage weight, images, scripts and app load on key templates
AnalyticsWhether key events are present and trustworthy
Search and discoverabilityIndexing, metadata, internal links, structured data and product data
TechnologyTheme condition, apps and integration risks

What you receive

A written summary with prioritised observations, key opportunities and recommended next actions. The exact format is agreed with the scope.

PriorityWhat it means
Fix nowLikely to be causing clear harm, and reasonable to fix quickly
Plan nextWorthwhile, but needs scoping or more evidence
InvestigateWorth testing or researching before spending on it
Leave for nowReal, but not worth the cost today

The conversation afterwards

We walk you through what we found and answer questions. If you want help with any of it, we can discuss that then. You are free to take the recommendations to your own team or another supplier.
