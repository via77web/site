CAPABILITY / ECOMMERCE DESIGN AND UX

Ecommerce design that helps people choose and buy.

A good-looking store still has to answer practical questions: what is this, will it suit me, what does it cost to get here? We design the journey and the interface together, with development in the room.

What does ecommerce UX and UI design cover?

UX decides how someone finds a product, compares options and reaches checkout. UI is how that looks and behaves on screen. On a Shopify project we design both as a set of reusable components rather than one-off page layouts, so the design survives real content and can be built within budget.

Who it suits

  • Brands launching a store and needing a considered experience from the start.
  • Shopify merchants redesigning without changing platform.
  • Teams whose product range has outgrown the original navigation.
  • Brands with strong identity that has not translated to their store.

When it becomes relevant

  • Customers ask questions the product page should answer.
  • Most traffic is on phones but the store was designed for desktop.
  • The range has grown and collections no longer make sense.
  • The brand looks different across the site, email and social.

What is included

  • A review of existing journeys and customer questions.
  • Navigation and collection structure.
  • Key templates: home, collection, product, cart and content pages.
  • Phone and desktop designs in Figma or a working prototype.
  • A component and style system ready for development.
  • Design notes covering states developers and editors need.

What depends on scope

  • Customer research, such as interviews or moderated testing.
  • The number of unique templates.
  • Photography, copywriting and brand development.
  • Accessibility review depth.
  • Checkout design, which depends on your Shopify plan.

Who does what

You provide

  • Brand guidelines and assets.
  • Real product data and photography where possible.
  • Customer questions from support or sales.
  • Feedback from one agreed decision maker.

VIA77 owns

  • Journey and interface design.
  • The component and style system.
  • Design rationale in plain language.
  • Support for the build, whoever develops it.

Platforms and third parties

  • Shopify sets checkout and platform limits.
  • Theme or app vendors control their components.
  • Your photographer or copywriter owns any work they supply.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of templates and components.
  • Depth of customer research.
  • Whether brand and content are ready.
  • Number of review rounds and stakeholders.

After launch

A launch is the first real test of a design. Review how people use the store, keep a list of what to change and update the component set rather than patching individual pages.

Our conversion optimisation work is the natural next stage once there is enough evidence.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Do you design in Figma?＋

Where it helps, yes. Some problems are better tested in a working prototype, especially filters and variant selection.

Can you design if another team builds it?＋

Yes. We will agree the handover format with your developers early so the design arrives in a form they can use.

Will a redesign increase our conversion rate?＋

We cannot promise that. A redesign can remove known problems, but results depend on traffic, products, pricing and many other factors. We can help you set a baseline so changes can be judged fairly.
