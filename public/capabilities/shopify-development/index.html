Build technically robust commerce platforms capable of scaling.

How does shopify development support commerce?

Build maintainable templates, structured content, accessible interactions and tested integrations around the agreed customer journey. The work connects customer needs to the operating requirements of the business. An engagement begins by defining the problem, the evidence available and the responsibilities of the people involved, then turns those decisions into a scoped plan with clear acceptance criteria.

SCOPE / OWNERSHIP / NEXT STEPS

When your theme is difficult to change

For brands launching on Shopify or replacing fragile templates and overlapping apps. Start with the customer task and the team that will maintain it.

Concrete deliverables

An agreed template and section inventory, theme implementation, accessible interaction states, app review, performance checks and a documented release checklist.

Who does what

Your team provides product data, content and account permissions. VIA77 owns the agreed build and handover; app vendors remain responsible for their services. Test in a development environment before release.

Investment, timing and support

Template variety, custom interactions, app dependencies and content readiness determine investment and timing. Agree a defect-support window and ongoing change ownership before launch.

A useful question: Is a custom theme always required?

No. Evaluate an existing theme against the requirements first. Custom development is justified where it resolves a specific experience or maintenance constraint.

USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How much does a Shopify website cost?＋

Scope drives the investment: catalogue complexity, UX, integrations, migration and ongoing support all matter. The project form uses indicative investment bands starting at A$15k–A$30k to guide the conversation; these are not fixed quotes.

How long does a project take?＋

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, system access and decision-making. Discovery establishes the dependencies and an achievable delivery plan before a launch date is committed.

Can VIA77 work with our existing ecommerce team?＋

Yes. The engagement can complement an internal team, with responsibilities, decision points and handover requirements agreed at the beginning.

What happens after launch?＋

A launch should include monitoring and a clear owner for operational issues. An ongoing optimisation engagement can then turn customer behaviour and research into a prioritised improvement roadmap.
