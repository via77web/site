CAPABILITY / SHOPIFY DEVELOPMENT

Shopify development your team can keep running.

We build and improve Shopify storefronts from Melbourne for brands across Australia. The aim is a store that suits your catalogue and that your team can change without calling a developer for every edit.

What does a Shopify developer do on a project like this?

Most of the work is turning decisions into maintainable theme code: templates, reusable sections, product and collection logic, cart behaviour and the connections to apps. We start by testing whether an existing theme can do the job, because custom code is only worth paying for when it solves a specific problem.

Who it suits

  • Brands launching on Shopify that want more than a default theme setup.
  • Shopify merchants whose theme has become slow or hard to change.
  • Marketing and ecommerce teams that need to publish pages without developer help.
  • Internal teams that need extra development capacity with clear boundaries.

When it becomes relevant

  • Simple content changes need a developer or break other pages.
  • Several apps do overlapping jobs and nobody is sure which can go.
  • The store looks fine on desktop but is awkward on a phone.
  • A redesign has been approved and needs to be built properly.

Existing theme or custom build?

ApproachUsually suitsTrade-off
Existing themeSmaller catalogues, standard journeys, faster launchesLower cost, but your store is bounded by the theme and updates need care
Adapted themeA strong base plus a few specific sections or behavioursGood balance, provided changes are kept tidy and documented
Custom themeDistinct brand experiences or unusual catalogue needsMost control, more upfront cost and you own more code long term

What is included

  • An agreed list of templates and sections.
  • Theme adaptation or custom theme development.
  • Reusable sections with settings your team can edit in the Shopify theme editor.
  • A review of installed apps: keep, replace or remove.
  • Mobile, keyboard and browser testing against an agreed checklist.
  • A release plan and written handover notes.

What depends on scope

  • How many unique templates the store needs.
  • Custom product logic such as bundles, configurators or gated pricing.
  • Whether content is ready or also needs producing.
  • Data migration, which is scoped under migration and replatforming.
  • Checkout changes, which depend on your Shopify plan.

Who does what

You provide

  • Shopify store access and collaborator permissions.
  • Final product data, content and imagery.
  • One person who can make decisions and sign off.
  • Details of any apps and third-party services you pay for.

VIA77 owns

  • The agreed build and its code quality.
  • Testing against the agreed checklist.
  • Release planning and handover notes.
  • Clear advice when something is out of scope.

Platforms and third parties

  • Shopify runs the platform, checkout and hosting.
  • App vendors own their apps, updates and support.
  • Payment and shipping providers own their services.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of templates and custom sections.
  • Custom interactions and product logic.
  • Number and quality of existing apps.
  • Content and data readiness.
  • How quickly feedback and sign-off arrive.

After launch

Agree a defect-support period before launch so everyone knows what counts as a defect and who fixes it. Theme updates, app changes and new features sit outside that period unless they are agreed separately.

If you want continuing development, ongoing improvement sets up a prioritised backlog.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Do we need a custom Shopify theme?＋

Often not. If an existing theme meets most requirements, adapting it costs less and is easier to maintain. A custom theme makes sense when your catalogue or brand needs behaviour a theme cannot provide cleanly.

Can you work on the theme we already have?＋

Usually, yes. We review the code first. If it is too fragile, we will explain the risk and compare fixing it with rebuilding it.

Are you a Shopify Plus Partner?＋

We do not currently claim Shopify Partner or Shopify Plus Partner status on this site. Ask us about the specifics of your project and we will tell you plainly what we can take on.

Why work with a Melbourne team?＋

You get time-zone overlap and the option of meeting in person where that helps. Most delivery works remotely, so we also work with brands elsewhere in Australia.
