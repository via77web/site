CAPABILITY / INTEGRATIONS

Shopify integrations with a clear owner for every piece of data.

Most integration problems are not about connecting two systems. They are about nobody deciding which system is right when the two disagree, and what happens when a sync fails on a busy day.

What is a commerce integration?

It is how Shopify exchanges information with the other systems your business runs on, such as inventory, finance, fulfilment, customer and marketing tools. We define each data flow first, then choose the simplest reliable method: a native connector, an established app, an integration tool or custom development.

Who it suits

  • Merchants re-keying orders, stock or customer details by hand.
  • Brands moving to Shopify with existing operational systems.
  • Teams whose current connections fail without anyone noticing.
  • Businesses adding a new system to an existing Shopify store.

When it becomes relevant

  • Stock levels differ between systems.
  • Orders need manual correction before they can ship.
  • Finance reconciles sales in spreadsheets.
  • Nobody knows who to call when a connection stops.

What is included

  • A map of systems and data flows.
  • A data-ownership decision for each key field.
  • A recommendation on connector, app, integration tool or custom build.
  • Field mapping and transformation rules.
  • Testing with realistic records, including failures.
  • Monitoring and escalation notes.

What depends on scope

  • The systems involved and the quality of their APIs and documentation.
  • Volume and how quickly data must update.
  • Historical data clean-up.
  • Custom logic and exception handling.
  • Access to vendors and their test environments.

Who does what

You provide

  • Access to each system and its documentation.
  • People who understand the current process.
  • Test records and a safe test environment where available.
  • Vendor contacts and licences.

VIA77 owns

  • The integration plan and agreed build.
  • Testing against agreed scenarios.
  • Documentation of flows and failure handling.
  • Clear statements of what we can and cannot control.

Platforms and third parties

  • Each vendor owns its platform, API, uptime and any changes to them.
  • App and connector vendors support their own products.
  • Shopify sets API limits and versions.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of systems and flows.
  • Quality of vendor APIs and documentation.
  • Custom rules and exceptions.
  • Data quality and clean-up.
  • Availability of test environments.

After launch

APIs change and businesses change. Someone needs to own monitoring, API version updates and the response when something fails. We agree that owner and the support boundary before launch, whether that is your team, a vendor or an ongoing arrangement with us.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Which systems do you integrate with?＋

It depends on the system and its API, so we review them for your project rather than publishing a list of logos. Tell us what you use and we will say early whether we are a good fit.

Should we use an app or a custom integration?＋

Start with a maintained app or connector if it handles your real scenarios. Custom work makes sense when it cannot, and when you are willing to own the upkeep.

What happens if a sync fails?＋

That should be decided before launch: how the failure is detected, whether it retries, who gets told and how records are corrected.
