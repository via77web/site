CAPABILITY / PRODUCT DATA AND AI DISCOVERY

Product information that people and machines can understand.

Search engines and AI assistants can only describe what they can read. The dependable place to start is your own product data: accurate, consistent and visible on the page.

What does AI commerce mean here?

We use it narrowly: preparing your catalogue so products are easier to find and describe across search and AI tools, and assessing whether an on-site assistant would actually help. We cannot control or guarantee whether any AI tool mentions your brand.

Who it suits

  • Catalogues with attributes customers filter or ask about.
  • Brands where products are hard to compare.
  • Teams being asked for an AI plan and wanting a sensible first step.

When it becomes relevant

  • Product titles and attributes follow no consistent rule.
  • Filters miss products because the data is incomplete.
  • Page content and structured data disagree.
  • Someone wants an AI assistant on the site before the data can support it.

What is included

  • An audit of product titles, descriptions and attributes.
  • A consistent attribute model using Shopify metafields where suitable.
  • Accurate product structured data in the theme.
  • Filter and collection recommendations.
  • An assessment of any proposed AI tool against your real catalogue.

What depends on scope

  • Catalogue size and data quality.
  • Where product data is currently owned.
  • Whether content rewriting is included.
  • Whether a third-party assistant or feed tool is involved.

Who does what

You provide

  • Product data, or access to the system that holds it.
  • Someone who knows the products.
  • Evidence for product claims.

VIA77 owns

  • The data model and theme implementation.
  • Structured data that matches visible content.
  • Plain advice on what is and is not known.

Platforms and third parties

  • Search engines and AI tools decide what they show.
  • Vendors own any assistant or feed tools.
  • Your business owns accuracy of product claims.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of products and categories.
  • Data quality.
  • Who does the content updates.

After launch

New products keep arriving, so the rules need to live with whoever manages the catalogue. A short documented standard does more good than a one-off clean-up.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Can you get us recommended by ChatGPT or other assistants?＋

We cannot promise that, and nobody can guarantee it. We can make your product information clear and accurate, which is the part you control.

Do we need an AI assistant on our store?＋

Often not yet. Better search, filters and product information may solve the same problem with less risk.

Does structured data improve rankings?＋

It helps search engines understand a page and may make it eligible for some search features. It is not a ranking shortcut, and it has to match what is visible on the page.
