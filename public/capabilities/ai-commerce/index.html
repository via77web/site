AI Commerce

Make products and content understandable across emerging customer journeys.

How does ai commerce support commerce?

Improve taxonomy, attributes, metadata and visible answer-first content; evaluate assisted discovery against the actual catalogue. The work connects customer needs to the operating requirements of the business. An engagement begins by defining the problem, the evidence available and the responsibilities of the people involved, then turns those decisions into a scoped plan with clear acceptance criteria.

SCOPE / OWNERSHIP / NEXT STEPS

When product information is hard to interpret

For catalogues with inconsistent attributes, unclear claims or weak discovery. Start with accurate visible information that customers and supported search systems can understand.

Concrete deliverables

A taxonomy and attribute audit, content-gap register, structured-data recommendations and an assisted-discovery prototype where there is a defined customer task.

Who does what

Your product specialists approve facts and claims. VIA77 structures the experience and tests representative questions; business owners decide where human review is required.

Investment, timing and support

Catalogue scale, source quality and evaluation coverage affect the work. Monitor incorrect answers and unsupported claims after release. No schema or special file can guarantee AI citations.

A useful question: Does this replace technical SEO?

No. Crawlability, useful content and credible evidence remain foundations. Improve those before investing in an experimental discovery interface.

USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How much does a Shopify website cost?＋

Scope drives the investment: catalogue complexity, UX, integrations, migration and ongoing support all matter. The project form uses indicative investment bands starting at A$15k–A$30k to guide the conversation; these are not fixed quotes.

How long does a project take?＋

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, system access and decision-making. Discovery establishes the dependencies and an achievable delivery plan before a launch date is committed.

Can VIA77 work with our existing ecommerce team?＋

Yes. The engagement can complement an internal team, with responsibilities, decision points and handover requirements agreed at the beginning.

What happens after launch?＋

A launch should include monitoring and a clear owner for operational issues. An ongoing optimisation engagement can then turn customer behaviour and research into a prioritised improvement roadmap.
