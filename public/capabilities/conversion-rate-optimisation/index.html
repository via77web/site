Conversion Rate Optimisation

Treat launch as the beginning rather than the end.

How does conversion rate optimisation support commerce?

Validate measurement, study customer behaviour, prioritise hypotheses and use an appropriate experiment or research method. The work connects customer needs to the operating requirements of the business. An engagement begins by defining the problem, the evidence available and the responsibilities of the people involved, then turns those decisions into a scoped plan with clear acceptance criteria.

SCOPE / OWNERSHIP / NEXT STEPS

When the funnel shows a problem but not its cause

For trading teams with unanswered questions about discovery, product choice or checkout. Check event accuracy and segment differences before interpreting a headline conversion rate.

Concrete deliverables

A measurement review, prioritised hypothesis backlog, experiment or research plan, implementation scope and a decision record describing results and uncertainty.

Who does what

Your team provides analytics access, trading context and campaign dates. VIA77 defines the method; both teams agree the primary measure and commercial guardrails before a change ships.

Investment, timing and support

Traffic, seasonality, purchase frequency and measurement quality govern timing. Lower-traffic stores may need task research instead of an underpowered A/B test. Ongoing scope follows the evidence.

A useful question: Can you guarantee a conversion uplift?

No. An experiment can be inconclusive or negative. The useful outcome is a defensible decision, including reverting a change when the evidence does not support it.
