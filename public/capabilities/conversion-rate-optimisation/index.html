CAPABILITY / CONVERSION OPTIMISATION

Conversion work that starts with evidence.

We check the numbers can be trusted, find out where and why people drop out, then prioritise what is worth changing. Some stores should run A/B tests. Many should not yet, and we will tell you which you are.

What is ecommerce conversion rate optimisation?

It is a structured way to remove reasons people do not buy. It looks at analytics, customer research and the store itself, then tests or ships changes in priority order. It does not guarantee a percentage increase, because results depend on traffic, offer, price and seasonality as well as the website.

Who it suits

  • Stores with steady traffic that want to improve what they already have.
  • Teams with plenty of ideas and no good way to rank them.
  • Brands unsure whether their analytics are accurate.
  • Merchants deciding between a targeted fix and a rebuild.

When it becomes relevant

  • Traffic is growing faster than sales.
  • A step in the funnel loses more people than expected.
  • Tests have been run without clear conclusions.
  • Decisions are made on opinion because the data is not trusted.

What is included

  • A review of analytics and event quality.
  • Funnel analysis by device and key segments.
  • Heuristic review of important pages.
  • Customer research where available, such as surveys, support questions or session recordings.
  • A prioritised list of hypotheses.
  • Implementation or test plans for agreed changes.

What depends on scope

  • How much research is possible.
  • Whether tracking must be fixed first.
  • Test tooling, where appropriate.
  • Who implements the changes.
01

Check measurement

Purchases, revenue and key steps must be recorded correctly before we draw conclusions.

02

Find where people drop out

We compare the funnel by device, channel and page type.

03

Find out why

Analytics show where. Research helps explain the reason.

04

Prioritise

Each idea gets a hypothesis, the evidence behind it, likely effort and how it will be judged.

05

Ship or test

Clear fixes get shipped. Uncertain changes get tested if the traffic can support it. Then we review what we learned.

Who does what

You provide

  • Analytics and Shopify access.
  • Support questions and customer feedback.
  • Sales context: promotions, stock issues and seasonality.
  • Someone who can approve changes.

VIA77 owns

  • The analysis and prioritised plan.
  • Agreed design and development changes.
  • Honest reporting, including inconclusive results.

Platforms and third parties

  • Analytics and testing vendors own their tools.
  • Consent settings and privacy decisions remain with your business.
  • Shopify limits what can change in checkout on some plans.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Traffic and order volume.
  • State of existing tracking.
  • Depth of research.
  • Whether changes are small or structural.

After launch

This work has no natural end, but it should have regular review points. If it is not producing useful decisions, change the plan or stop. Ongoing improvement describes how we run it over time.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

When is a store ready for A/B testing?＋

When it has enough conversions on the page being tested to reach a reliable result in a reasonable time. Lower-traffic stores usually do better with research, fixing clear problems and watching before-and-after trends carefully.

How much uplift can we expect?＋

We do not forecast a number. Be wary of anyone who promises one before seeing your data.

Do you need our analytics access?＋

Yes, read access at minimum. If tracking is unreliable, fixing it comes first.
