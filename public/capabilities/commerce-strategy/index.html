CAPABILITY / ECOMMERCE STRATEGY

Decide what to build before you pay to build it.

Strategy here means something practical: a shared view of the goal, the customer, the constraints and the order of work. It should make the next decisions easier, not produce a slide deck that sits in a folder.

What does ecommerce strategy include?

It connects business goals to website decisions: who you are selling to, how the range is organised, what the store must do at launch, what can wait and which systems are involved. It is usually the first stage of a launch or migration, and can also be a short standalone engagement.

Who it suits

  • Founders preparing to launch online.
  • Established brands deciding whether to replatform.
  • Teams whose stakeholders want different things from the site.
  • Businesses adding a new channel, region or customer type.

When it becomes relevant

  • There are several competing requests and no agreed order.
  • You are about to ask for quotes but cannot yet describe the scope.
  • Someone has proposed a new platform without a clear reason.
  • Sales are flat and the cause is unclear.

What is included

  • Stakeholder conversations and a review of available data.
  • Customer and range review.
  • Requirements for launch and later.
  • Platform and technology considerations.
  • A prioritised roadmap with dependencies and risks.
  • A brief that designers and developers can quote against.

What depends on scope

  • Whether customer research is included.
  • Competitor and market review depth.
  • Number of stakeholders and business units.
  • How much data is available.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of stakeholders.
  • Business complexity.
  • Available data and research depth.

After launch

A plan is only useful if someone owns it. We recommend reviewing it when the first stage is complete and when your business situation changes.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Can we do strategy without committing to a build?＋

Yes. The output should be useful to any team you choose.

Is this the same as a Commerce Assessment?＋

No. An assessment reviews an existing store and ranks what to improve. Strategy sets the direction, and also works if you have no store yet.

Do we need this for a small store?＋

Not always. A short conversation may be enough, and we will say so.
