03/ How delivery works

Seven stages.
A clear output from each.

  1. 01DiscoverUnderstand customers, goals, systems and constraints. You get: A shared brief.
  2. 02DefineAgree priorities and requirements. You get: Scope, site structure and a plan.
  3. 03DesignDesign for phones and desktop. You get: Reusable page and component designs.
  4. 04DevelopBuild the store and required integrations. You get: A tested development store.
  5. 05DeployMove data, test and launch. You get: A launch checklist and handover notes.
  6. 06LearnReview how real customers use the store. You get: An evidence-based list of fixes.
  7. 07ImproveShip changes in priority order. You get: A maintained backlog and change record.
