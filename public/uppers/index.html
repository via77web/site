Four things on the wrapper. All of them in the bar.

The list on the front of the pack is the recipe, not a flavour note.

  • +Kakadu plum

    A native fruit from the Top End with a sharp, sour bite. It cuts through the chocolate so the bar doesn't go cloying.

  • +Macadamia nuts

    Roasted and chopped, for a softer, buttery crunch under the puffed rice.

  • +Walnuts & blueberries

    Walnut for depth, dried blueberry for a chewy bit of sweetness in most bites.

  • +Rice bubbles

    The whole point. Puffed rice bound with protein and coated in milk chocolate.
