Privacy information

How this website handles information.

Project and assessment forms

While delivery is unavailable, this website prepares a text file on your device. Form contents remain in page memory and are not sent to VIA77, saved to browser storage or stored in a database. Moving between steps preserves your answers. Refreshing or leaving the page clears them. A downloaded brief is a file under your control.

Analytics

No analytics credentials or third-party analytics scripts are configured. The event abstraction excludes form contents and contact details; it emits local browser events only unless a separately configured analytics integration is added.

Site delivery

The hosting service processes the requests needed to deliver this website. Fonts use your device’s system fonts. External reference links take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Before enquiry delivery is enabled

The legal business identity, privacy contact, enquiry providers and retention arrangements need to be confirmed before live data collection. This preview notice must then be reviewed against the actual operating arrangements.

When enquiry delivery is enabled

The form will say “Send enquiry” and disclose that information is sent to VIA77. Cloudflare Turnstile will check submissions for spam, and Resend will process the email. Form data is not written to a site database or included in analytics events. VIA77 must confirm its recipient, legal identity, privacy contact and mailbox retention arrangements before enabling this mode.
