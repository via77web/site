WEBSITE INFORMATION

Privacy information

How this website handles information.

Enquiry and assessment forms

The forms ask for your name, email and project details. The button on the form shows what it will do.

If it says “Prepare your brief”, nothing is sent to VIA77. The details stay on your device and you can download them as a text file.

If it says “Send enquiry” or “Request an assessment”, your details are sent to VIA77 so we can respond. Cloudflare Turnstile checks the submission for spam. Supabase stores the enquiry in a private database in Sydney, which only VIA77 directors can access, and Resend emails them a notification. Enquiry contents are not included in analytics.

Analytics

No third-party analytics scripts are currently installed on this website. The site has a small event layer that excludes form contents and contact details. It sends nothing to a third party unless an analytics tool is added and this page is updated.

Website delivery

Cloudflare hosts this website and processes the requests needed to deliver it. Demo stores may load fonts and scripts from public code libraries. Camera features in the glassi demo run only if you choose to use them.

External links

Links to other services take you to websites with their own privacy policies.
