Magento to Shopify: what does a migration actually require?

A migration is not a database copy. Here is what to plan, and what will not transfer unchanged.

Published by VIA77. Last reviewed 5 November 2026.

The short answer

A Magento to Shopify migration needs an audit of what you trade on today, mapped data, a redirect plan, replacements for custom extensions and a full rehearsal. Expect some manual work: complex configurable products may need review and customer passwords cannot be migrated.

What does a Magento to Shopify migration involve?

A migration combines catalogue and customer-data mapping, storefront design, URL planning, integrations and a controlled operational change. It is not a direct copy of one database into another. Establish which records and behaviours the business needs, which can transfer and which need a different implementation.

Audit what is trading today

Inventory products, variants, attributes, categories, content, integrations and extensions. Identify business rules hidden in custom code or manual spreadsheets. Speak with fulfilment, customer service and finance teams as well as the people who manage the storefront.

Map data before moving it

Create field mappings and representative samples. Check variant structure, images, inventory, prices and customer details. Keep a record of unsupported fields and decide how they will be handled. Customer login continuity needs a deliberate communication plan; do not assume account credentials transfer unchanged.

Protect discovery with a URL plan

Inventory valuable existing URLs and map each to the closest useful destination. Preserve relevant content and metadata where appropriate. Test redirect chains, canonical tags, internal links and sitemap coverage. Search visibility can fluctuate during a migration; no responsible plan guarantees that rankings will remain unchanged.

Rehearse complete orders

Use realistic catalogue and fulfilment scenarios in a test environment. Check inventory changes, discounts, payments, tax configuration, shipping, customer notifications and refunds where applicable. Confirm that each connected system receives the expected event and that failed events can be investigated.

Make launch a controlled decision

Agree a content freeze, final data import, DNS change, validation checklist and named launch owner. Define the conditions for stopping or rolling back before launch night. Keep the old platform available as required by the transition plan and retention obligations.

Watch the business after the switch

Monitor orders, fulfilment, payment reconciliation, error reports and search coverage. Compare against a baseline while accounting for campaign activity, stock availability and seasonality. Keep a prioritised backlog of improvements rather than treating release as the end of the engagement.

Facts to plan around

  • Passwords: customer passwords cannot be imported, so plan account activation or reset messages.
  • Products: Magento configurable products map to Shopify variants, but complex setups may need manual review. Imported products can be incomplete, so check them.
  • Orders: Shopify’s customer CSV import does not bring order information. Import any history last, after products and customers.
  • Redirects: Shopify allows up to 100,000 redirects, or 20 million on Plus. They work only where the old URL no longer loads a page, and some paths are reserved.
  • Sender domain: set up SPF and DKIM so store emails are less likely to end up in spam.

Sources: Shopify customer import, URL redirects, migration checklist and Shopify’s Magento guide, checked 5 November 2026.
