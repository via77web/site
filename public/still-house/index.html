STILL HOUSE

STILL HOUSE explores the distance between seeing a piece of furniture and knowing it belongs in your home. Space, material and proportion lead the story.

Home & furniture / Considered product presentation and high-consideration purchasing. / Platform: interface concept, not a deployed store.

DESIGN INTENT / NOT MEASURED RESULTS

The customer problem

A furniture buyer needs to judge scale, finish and delivery constraints before buying a high-consideration object. The fictional brief centres on reducing uncertainty about fit within a room.

01 / Context and detail

Alternate room photography with material and product-detail views. In production, pair these with visible measurements, finish choices and access requirements near the purchase action.

The trade-off

Atmospheric photography helps people imagine the product but cannot replace a dimensional drawing or accurate delivery promise. The design reserves space for those facts rather than manufacturing them for a concept.

Proposed validation

Ask participants to establish whether the item fits a given space and describe the delivery requirements. Compare confidence with factual accuracy; test unclear measurements before evaluating conversion. No client or measured result is implied.

01

Challenge

Will it fit my space, and what should I know before committing?

02

Opportunity

This design exploration asks how clearer product information could make the next customer decision easier. No customer research finding is claimed.

03

Strategy

The concept starts with an architectural setting. A complete store would follow with accessible dimensions, material care, delivery lead times and installation requirements. Those details help turn an emotional response into an informed decision.

04

Experience

Considered product presentation and high-consideration purchasing. The concept explores a coherent journey across desktop and mobile.

05

Technology

A production implementation would define catalogue ownership, inventory and order events, payments and analytics before selecting integrations.

06

Outcome

An original interface exploration, not a measured commercial result. This concept has not been deployed for a client; no conversion uplift, revenue impact or customer-research result is claimed.
