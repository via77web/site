Our story

We started with one kiln and a very full car boot.

Fern & Field began as a weekend market stall. We drove between studios, bought what we'd use ourselves, and realised most of the things we loved had no proper home online.

So we built one. Every piece lists the maker, the materials and what to do if it breaks. We'd rather sell you fewer things that last.
