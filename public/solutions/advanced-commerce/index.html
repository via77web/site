SOLUTION / CUSTOM FUNCTIONALITY

When a standard store setup is not enough.

Sometimes your products, pricing or customers need something a theme and an app cannot do cleanly. We help you work out whether that is true, then build the smallest thing that solves it.

What counts as advanced commerce?

Requirements that fall outside a standard setup, such as product configurators, complex bundles, trade or wholesale rules, interactive product views or custom logic in the buying journey. This page is about the business situation. Integrations covers connections between systems.

Who it suits

  • Products that need configuring before purchase.
  • Businesses selling to both consumers and trade.
  • Brands wanting interactive product experiences.
  • Stores relying on awkward workarounds.

When it becomes relevant

  • Staff handle exceptions manually on most orders.
  • Several apps have been stacked to mimic one feature.
  • Customers cannot work out which option they need.
  • A new business model does not fit the current store.

What is included

  • Requirements and a build, buy or change-the-process decision.
  • A prototype of the riskiest part where useful.
  • Agreed custom development.
  • Testing, including edge cases and failure states.
  • Documentation and ownership notes.

What depends on scope

  • Your Shopify plan, because some features are plan-dependent.
  • Complexity of rules and exceptions.
  • Performance needs, especially for 3D or media.
  • Whether integrations are also involved.

Who does what

You provide

  • A clear description of rules and exceptions.
  • Real examples, including awkward ones.
  • Access to relevant accounts.

VIA77 owns

  • Agreed custom development.
  • Testing and documentation.
  • Advice when building is not the right answer.

Platforms and third parties

  • Shopify sets platform and plan limits.
  • App vendors control their products.
  • Device and browser makers affect features such as AR.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of rules and exceptions.
  • Integration needs.
  • Media and performance requirements.
  • Long-term maintenance expectations.

After launch

Custom features need owners. Plan for platform changes, browser updates and new requirements. If nobody will own a feature, reconsider building it.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

Do we need Shopify Plus for this?＋

Sometimes. Some checkout and B2B features depend on the plan. Our Shopify vs Shopify Plus guide explains how to assess that.

Should we go headless?＋

Rarely as a first step. It adds cost and ownership. We would want a specific requirement that a theme cannot meet.

Could an app do this instead?＋

Possibly, and we check. Apps are often the better option when they cover your real scenarios.
