SOLUTION / Advanced Commerce

When standard ecommerce isn’t enough.

Complex commerce should still feel simple to the customer. VIA77 designs and engineers commerce systems for businesses with advanced operational, customer and technology requirements.

What is advanced commerce?

Advanced commerce connects a storefront to the operational systems and customer requirements that sit beyond a standard online catalogue. It can include ERP and PIM integrations, B2B purchasing, subscriptions, multi-market selling and custom applications. VIA77 starts with the workflow and its constraints, then defines the smallest reliable architecture that supports the business without adding unnecessary complexity.

SCOPE / OWNERSHIP / NEXT STEPS

When systems disagree about an order

For teams reconciling inventory by hand or repeating product and customer updates across systems. Begin with one complete workflow and its exceptions.

Concrete deliverables

A source-of-truth map, API and event specification, field transformations, retry rules, reconciliation checks and a support runbook with named owners.

Who does what

Your ERP, PIM or 3PL owner provides documentation and test access. VIA77 implements the agreed connection; each vendor validates its own system behaviour and limits.

Investment, timing and support

API limits, sandbox availability, event volume and legacy data quality determine timing and investment. Monitoring must distinguish delayed events from failed or duplicated operations.

A useful question: Should every update be real time?

No. Choose event-driven updates or scheduled reconciliation based on stock risk and operational need. Faster synchronisation can add cost and more failure states.

USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

Can VIA77 integrate our ERP?＋

Feasibility depends on the ERP, available APIs, data ownership, licensing and the workflows in scope. We assess those constraints before committing to an integration approach.

How long does a project take?＋

Timing depends on scope, content readiness, system access and decision-making. Discovery establishes the dependencies and an achievable delivery plan before a launch date is committed.

Can VIA77 work with our existing ecommerce team?＋

Yes. The engagement can complement an internal team, with responsibilities, decision points and handover requirements agreed at the beginning.

What happens after launch?＋

A launch should include monitoring and a clear owner for operational issues. An ongoing optimisation engagement can then turn customer behaviour and research into a prioritised improvement roadmap.
