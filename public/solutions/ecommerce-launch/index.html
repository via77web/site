SOLUTION / ECOMMERCE LAUNCH

Launching a Shopify store, without leaving the hard parts until last.

You have a product and a business to run. We bring planning, design and development together so payments, shipping, tax and content are sorted before launch week, not during it.

What does launching a Shopify store involve?

More than a theme and some products. A launch connects your offer, product data, store design, payments, shipping, tax settings, emails, analytics and the operations behind each order. The engagement covers those decisions in order and tests the full buying journey before opening.

Who it suits

  • New brands selling online for the first time.
  • Established businesses adding ecommerce.
  • Brands moving from a marketplace or social selling to their own store.
  • Founders who want one team across design and development.

When it becomes relevant

  • You have product and brand but no store plan.
  • You have tried setting up a store and stalled on the details.
  • You have a launch date and need the work sequenced.
  • You are comparing a simple theme with something more custom.

What is included

  • Planning: audience, range, launch requirements and what can wait.
  • Store structure, navigation and page design.
  • Shopify setup and theme adaptation or custom build.
  • Product, collection and content setup, as agreed.
  • Payments, shipping, tax and notification configuration with your input.
  • Analytics setup, launch testing and handover.

What depends on scope

  • Brand identity, photography and copywriting.
  • Number of products and how complex they are.
  • Custom functionality, subscriptions or bundles.
  • Integrations with stock, fulfilment or finance systems.
  • Multiple markets or currencies.

Who does what

You provide

  • Products, pricing and accurate product claims.
  • Brand assets, images and copy.
  • Accounts for Shopify, payments, shipping and analytics.
  • Policies, tax and legal decisions, with your advisers.

VIA77 owns

  • Project plan and agreed scope.
  • Design and development.
  • Testing against an agreed checklist.
  • Launch support and handover.

Platforms and third parties

  • Shopify runs the platform and checkout.
  • Payment and shipping providers own their approvals and services.
  • App vendors own their apps.

What affects cost and timing

We scope and quote after discovery rather than publishing a fixed price, because these factors move the work most:

  • Number of products and variants.
  • Existing theme or custom design.
  • Whether brand, photography and copy are ready.
  • Custom functionality and integrations.
  • How quickly decisions and approvals happen.

After launch

Plan for changes after launch. Real orders reveal things no test can. Agree the defect-support period, who handles day-to-day store updates, and when you will review early results.

QUESTIONS BUYERS ASK

Before you commit.

How long does it take to launch a Shopify store?＋

That depends on scope and how ready your content is. We confirm dependencies during planning, before committing to a launch date.

How much does it cost?＋

Scope drives the price: template count, custom features, integrations and content. Our guide to ecommerce website cost factors explains what to budget for, and we quote after discovery.

Can we start small and add later?＋

Yes, and we often recommend it. Launch what you need to sell well, then add features based on what customers do.

Who owns the store?＋

Your business should own the Shopify account, domains and data. We work through collaborator access.
