SOLUTION / Shopify Growth & Replatform

Change platform without losing what already works.

Replatforming isn’t simply rebuilding the same website somewhere else. It is an opportunity to remove technical debt, improve customer experience and create a better foundation for growth. But it also introduces risk. VIA77 approaches migration as a business, data, SEO and customer-experience project—not just a development exercise.

What does a Shopify migration involve?

A Shopify migration moves the catalogue, customer information, content and necessary operational workflows from an existing platform into a new commerce architecture. It includes data mapping, customer-experience decisions, URL redirects, integrations, testing and launch monitoring. The plan must distinguish what can transfer directly from what needs rebuilding, with a rehearsal and acceptance criteria before the trading business switches platforms.

01

Platform assessment

Understand what should move and what should not. Review Magento, WooCommerce, custom platforms and older Shopify implementations against the actual business requirements.

02

Data migration

Plan URL mapping, redirects, metadata, canonical checks, internal links and indexation.

Validate ERP, payments, CRM, email, inventory and 3PL workflows with the people responsible for them.

05

Customer experience redesign

Do not reproduce outdated UX simply because it existed on the old platform.

06

Controlled launch

Testing, migration validation, tracking, redirect verification and post-launch monitoring. Agree a rollback decision and launch ownership before release.

SCOPE / OWNERSHIP / NEXT STEPS

When the current platform creates friction

For stores moving from Magento, WooCommerce or a custom system. Migration engineering is the data and release workstream within a broader replatforming programme.

Concrete deliverables

A data-field mapping, import sample, redirect inventory, exception log, rehearsal report and cutover checklist. Validate record counts and representative customer journeys, not only a successful import message.

Who does what

Your team approves retained records and content. VIA77 maps and tests the move; system owners validate their integrations. Passwords, historic orders and app data require explicit feasibility checks.

Investment, timing and support

Data quality, URL volume, custom business rules and the cutover window affect scope. Agree rollback criteria, a content freeze and post-launch order and search monitoring.

A useful question: Can every record move unchanged?

Do not assume so. Confirm import support and data limits before quoting; decide how unsupported fields, accounts and historical records will be handled.

USEFUL QUESTIONS

Before we begin.

How much does a Shopify website cost?＋

Scope drives the investment: catalogue complexity, UX, integrations, migration and ongoing support all matter. The project form uses indicative investment bands starting at A$15k–A$30k to guide the conversation; these are not fixed quotes.

Will migration affect SEO?＋

URLs, content and platform behaviour can change during a migration, so search visibility may fluctuate. A URL inventory, redirect map, retained useful content and post-launch monitoring reduce avoidable disruption; rankings cannot be guaranteed.

Can VIA77 work with our existing ecommerce team?＋

Yes. The engagement can complement an internal team, with responsibilities, decision points and handover requirements agreed at the beginning.

What happens after launch?＋

A launch should include monitoring and a clear owner for operational issues. An ongoing optimisation engagement can then turn customer behaviour and research into a prioritised improvement roadmap.
