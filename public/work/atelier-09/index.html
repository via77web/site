ATELIER 09

ATELIER 09 explores how a fashion storefront can feel editorial without making shopping an afterthought. The product is the focal point; structure provides the clarity.

Fashion & accessories / Collection discovery, merchandising and product storytelling. / Platform: interface concept, not a deployed store.

DESIGN INTENT / NOT MEASURED RESULTS

The customer problem

A fashion shopper needs to move from an editorial outfit to the specific available garment, size and fit information. This concept explores the gap between inspiration and a purchase decision.

01 / Editorial to collection

Pair a large outfit story with direct collection navigation. Preserve product names and availability when moving into the grid so the campaign does not become a dead end.

The trade-off

Large photography communicates fit and styling but reduces the number of products visible at once. A real catalogue would test a denser grid and useful filters rather than assuming one layout suits every shopper.

Proposed validation

Ask shoppers to identify a garment from the campaign, locate a preferred size and explain the return policy. Record task failures and missing fit questions before choosing analytics or experiment measures. No research findings are claimed.

01

Challenge

What does it feel like, what does it pair with, and is it right for me?

02

Opportunity

This design exploration asks how clearer product information could make the next customer decision easier. No customer research finding is claimed.

03

Strategy

Tactile photography communicates material and form. A production journey would pair it with dimensions, on-body scale, availability and delivery information. Collections would use consistent product naming and useful filters rather than an endless campaign feed.

04

Experience

Collection discovery, merchandising and product storytelling. The concept explores a coherent journey across desktop and mobile.

05

Technology

A production implementation would define catalogue ownership, inventory and order events, payments and analytics before selecting integrations.

06

Outcome

An original interface exploration, not a measured commercial result. This concept has not been deployed for a client; no conversion uplift, revenue impact or customer-research result is claimed.
