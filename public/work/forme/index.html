WORK / BEAUTY / SKINCARE

FORME

A small skincare range with a clear daily routine and a set.

Commerce conceptOpen the demo store↗

Brief and audience

A skincare range with a serum, an elixir, a crème and a set. The aim was a store where the order of a daily routine is the main way to shop.

Intended audience: People building a simple skincare routine who want to understand what each product is for.

Status and role

Status: commerce concept. Not a client project. It takes no real orders or payments, and no results are claimed.

VIA77’s role: brand direction, interface design and front-end build. It is a front-end demonstration and is not connected to a live Shopify store.

Design and technical decisions

  • Sell one size of each product to reduce choices at the point of purchase.
  • Offer a set with a clear saving, so a whole routine is one decision.
  • Use a short looped film in the hero, with a lighter crop for phones and a still image when reduced motion is on.

Constraints and trade-offs

  • A video hero adds page weight, so it pauses off screen and has a poster image as a fallback.
  • The checkout is a demonstration. No payment details are requested.
  • The range is small, so filters do little work here.

What it demonstrates

  • Reusable store components
  • Quick view, product pages, bag and checkout flow
  • Motion that respects reduced-motion settings
