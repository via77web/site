STILL HOUSE

Considered purchases, with material options that change the price.

Commerce conceptOpen the demo store↗

Brief and audience

A furniture and objects store for considered, higher-value purchases, where material and size options change the price.

Intended audience: People furnishing a home, who need confidence about size, material and delivery before buying.

Status and role

Status: commerce concept. Not a client project. It takes no real orders or payments, and no results are claimed.

VIA77’s role: brand direction, interface design and front-end build. It is a front-end demonstration and is not connected to a live Shopify store.

Design and technical decisions

  • Update the price as soon as an option changes, so there are no surprises in the bag.
  • Add a section on whether a piece will fit, because that is a main reason to hesitate.
  • Keep the delivery conditions visible at the top of the store.

Constraints and trade-offs

  • A real furniture store needs delivery zones, lead times and possibly quote-based items, which are not modelled here.
  • Prices and products are illustrative.
  • Room-fit help is content only, not a tool.

What it demonstrates

  • Price-changing variants
  • Pre-purchase reassurance
  • Long-consideration page design
