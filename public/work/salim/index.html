WORK / MEN'S SKINCARE / SINGLE PRODUCT

Salim

A single-product store with one-off and refill purchase options.

Commerce conceptOpen the demo store↗

Brief and audience

A single-product store for a men’s day and night face cream, with a choice between one tube and a refill every eight weeks.

Intended audience: Men who want a simple skincare step and little to think about.

Status and role

Status: commerce concept. Not a client project. It takes no real orders or payments, and no results are claimed.

VIA77’s role: brand direction, interface design and front-end build. It is a front-end demonstration and is not connected to a live Shopify store.

Design and technical decisions

  • Put the purchase choice in one clear place: one tube or a discounted refill.
  • Explain ingredients and their purpose in plain language.
  • Label a simulated skin comparison clearly as simulated, rather than as a real before-and-after.

Constraints and trade-offs

  • Images are embedded in one large page, which is simple to host but heavy to load. A live build would serve them separately.
  • Claims about ingredients and testing would need evidence on a real product.
  • Contact and refill flows are demonstration only.

What it demonstrates

  • One-product store structure
  • Purchase option design
  • Honest labelling of simulated imagery
